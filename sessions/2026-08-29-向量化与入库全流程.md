## 关键发现(给 AI 自己看,防失忆)

### 入库主函数 processChunks(knowledge_process.go:244)
- **12步**:幂等清理→中断检查#1→建DB Chunk(父先子后)→中断检查#2→写chunks表(Always)→中断检查#3→建IndexInfo(3层拼接)→存储配额检查→BatchIndex→状态置processing入队→异步fan-out→FinalizeSubtask收敛
- **3个中断检查点**(L257/L468/L556):长任务防中途删/取消。deleting清数据,cancelled保留数据。不是冗余是长任务可中断标准做法。
- **chunks表Always写**(L479 注释L473-475):不管开不开向量库都写,wiki/graph/summary依赖chunks表。★chunks表是事实库,向量库是检索索引,两者分离。
- **父块先建拿真ID,子块挂ParentChunkID**(L376/432):父块ChunkType=ParentText无面包屑,子块ChunkType=Text有面包屑。父子模式子块不串prev/next(L455)。

### 3层拼接(knowledge_index_content.go:12 + chunk.go:202)
- `buildKnowledgeIndexContent(knowledge, chunk.EmbeddingContent())`
- 第1层文档标题knowledge.Title用\n(单)
- 第2层面包屑ContextHeader用\n\n(双)
- 第3层正文Content已TrimSpace
- 展开形态:<标题>\n<面包屑>\n\n<正文trim>
- ★标题(文档级,Knowledge表)vs面包屑(章节级,Tier1切时从Markdown标题提取,heading_splitter.go:72-115)不是同一个东西不重复
- ★Content要TrimSpace原因:边界切在换行处带首尾空行会稀释向量+浪费token,但Content字段本身不改保持End-Start==len不变量
- ★custom metadata不进embedding(文档级喂回答/摘要模型一次,不每chunk重复,避免稀释)

### BatchIndex(knowledge_process.go:566 → composite.go:212 → keywords_vector_hybrid_indexer.go:87)
- 调用链:composite去重+并发分发→KV引擎按retrieverType分支
- **批量+并发**:BatchEmbedWithPool(batch.go:27)按BATCH_EMBED_SIZE(默认5)分批→ants协程池并发→model.BatchEmbed(HTTP一次多条)
- **退避重试5次**(200/400/800/1600/3200ms)
- **sanitizeForEmbedding**(L159-175):base64图片→[image],超长截断20000rune
- **分批40并发写**:concurrentBatchSave→indexRepository.BatchSave(params["embedding"])
- **向量存储**:经additionalParams["embedding"](map[SourceID][]float32)传入。PG存pgvector.HalfVector(fp16,structs.go:27),ES存[]float32。向量库/关键词库同表字段级分离(Embedding列vs Content列)
- **开关NeedsEmbeddingModel**:VectorEnabled||KeywordEnabled任一true。实际生成向量看retrieverTypes含VectorRetrieverType:只Vector→embed+写向量列;只Keyword→不embed只写content靠全文检索;都开→一次embed同写一行;都不开→L617 skipStage不调BatchIndex
- ★注意CapVector/CapKeyword(capabilities.go)是agent工具层能力标记不是索引写入开关
- **失败回滚**(L566-593):标失败→删已写chunks→DeleteByKnowledgeIDList回滚向量库→failStage。错误码区分写失败vs限流(isLikelyRateLimitError)
- **配额检查**(L531-551):EstimateStorageSize用零向量估算不真调API,不足直接失败return不BatchIndex

### 异步后处理 + 状态机(本篇重点)
- **入库成功≠可用**,三态:processing→finalizing→completed
- processing:入库成功置(L627 finalizeIndexedKnowledgeState),不直接completed因为摘要/问题/图谱没跑
- finalizing:KnowledgePostProcess编排器SetFinalizing写pending_subtasks_count=N(knowledge.go:639-649原子)。★同步解析与异步富化唯一持久化交接点
- completed:FinalizeSubtask(repository/knowledge.go:571-628)计数-1归零才提升,WHERE守卫防cancel/delete被晚到promote覆盖
- **入队只入1个**(L662):TypeKnowledgePostProcess,真正fan-out在KnowledgePostProcessService.Handle(knowledge_post_process.go:78)
- **fan-out全异步**(asynq独立worker池):摘要QueueSummary/问题QueueQuestion/图谱QueueGraph/wiki QueueWiki/打标签(不占finalizing计数)
- ★★隐藏的二次BatchIndex:问题生成processQuestionGenerationForChunks(knowledge_process.go:1756-2074)把每chunk生成问句再建索引(L2062-2069第二次BatchIndex)。question-based检索增强,问句与原文两种语义分布各建锚点,SourceID指原chunk。向量库实际写两次(主+问题)
- **图谱抽取读chunks表不回源**:ExtractChunk(extract.go:230)GetChunkByID读chunks表→Extract喂LLM→AddGraph写Neo4j,节点挂Chunks=[chunk.ID]。前置门NEO4J_ENABLE!=true不入队(extract.go:99)
- **5阶段时间线**(knowledge_span.go:42-48):DocReader→Chunking→Embedding→Multimodal→PostProcess。DAG依赖StageDependencies(L70-76),fail级联cancel下游。beginStage/endStage/failStage/skipStage写knowledge_processing_spans表(knowledge.go:253-298)
